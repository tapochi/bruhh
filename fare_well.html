Ett

for the Sydney i once knew

This is one of the hardest letters I’ve ever had to write because it’s about something I never thought I’d have to say. I’ve been carrying a lot of thoughts and feelings since we ended things, and I realize that pretending nothing happened when we see each other isn’t fair — to either of us. I want to be honest, both with you and with myself, even if it’s difficult.

I know I hurt you by leaving, and I feel the weight of that. You were ready to be with me, and I couldn’t. That decision was mine alone, and it doesn’t change the fact that I care deeply about you and the time we shared. I’m sorry for the pain I caused, and I take responsibility for my choice.

Loving you has been one of the most intense and meaningful experiences of my life. We’ve shared moments that I will always treasure, and I will never forget the ways you made me feel loved and alive.

Our relationship at that time became complicated in ways that I can’t ignore, and as much as I love you, I realize that staying together isn’t healthy for either of us anymore. I can’t continue in a situation that leaves both of us hurt, frustrated, or unsure of where we stand. This isn’t about blame — it’s about truth. Sometimes love, no matter how fierce, isn’t enough to keep two souls together.

After we broke up, I met someone new, trying to fill the emptiness I felt — trying to cling to someone to numb the heartbreak. But it didn’t help. No one could replace what we had, and I realized that I needed to face my own feelings honestly. Leaving her wasn’t about her — it was about me, and the truth I had been avoiding.

Even though we’re no longer together, I hope we can both find peace and happiness in our own ways. I will always remember the love, the laughter, and the lessons we shared. You deserve someone who can fully be there for you, and I hope one day you find that.

Our love was real, beautiful, and unforgettable, but sometimes even love isn’t enough. I hope, one day, you can forgive me, and I hope you find the happiness you deserve. I’ll always remember you, and I’ll always love you, quietly, from afar.

Please always take care of yourself

Francis | Till we meet again
